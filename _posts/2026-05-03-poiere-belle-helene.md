<div style="font-family: 'Georgia', serif; font-size: 1.1em; color: #333; line-height: 1.7; max-width: 100%;">

    <h3 style="color: #d35400; border-bottom: 2px solid #eee; padding-bottom: 5px;">🍐 Ingredients</h3>
    <ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 20px;">
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>4 Pears</strong> (firm varieties like Santa Maria or Bosc)</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>2 cups</strong> Water & <strong>2 cups</strong> Granulated Sugar</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>1</strong> Lemon (juice)</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>1 packet</strong> Vanilla sugar (or 1 tsp vanilla extract)</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>150-250g</strong> Dark Chocolate</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>100-150ml</strong> Heavy Cream</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>1 tbsp</strong> Butter (optional, for shine)</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>Vanilla Ice Cream</strong> & <strong>Sliced Almonds</strong> (for serving)</li>
    </ul>

    <hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid #eee; margin: 30px 0;">

    <h3 style="color: #d35400; border-bottom: 2px solid #eee; padding-bottom: 5px;">👨‍🍳 Instructions</h3>
    <ol style="padding-left: 20px;">
        <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>Prepare the Pears:</strong> Peel the pears while keeping the stems intact. Slice a small piece off the bottom so they can stand upright.</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>Poach:</strong> In a large pot, bring the water, sugar, lemon juice, and vanilla to a boil. Add the pears and simmer on low heat for about <strong>15-20 minutes</strong> until tender.</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>Make the Sauce:</strong> Heat the cream in a small pan (do not boil). Remove from heat, add chopped chocolate, and stir until smooth. Add butter if using.</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>Serving:</strong> Drain the pears and place them on a serving plate. Add a scoop of vanilla ice cream, pour the warm chocolate sauce over the top, and garnish with toasted almonds.</li>
    </ol>

    <hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid #eee; margin: 30px 0;">

    <div style="background-color: #fff9eb; padding: 20px; border-left: 5px solid #f5a623; border-radius: 8px; margin-top: 20px;">
        <strong style="color: #f5a623;">💡 Pro Tip:</strong><br>
        For the best flavor, let the pears <strong>cool down inside the syrup</strong> so they fully absorb the sweetness.
    </div>
