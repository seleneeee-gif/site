    <h2 style="color: #c0392b;">🍐 Pears Belle Hélène</h2>

    <h3 style="color: #c0392b; border-bottom: 1px solid #f1e4d1; padding-bottom: 5px;">🛒 Ingredients</h3>
    <ul style="margin-bottom: 25px; padding-left: 20px;">
        <li>4 Ripe Pears (peeled but with stems)</li>
        <li>150g Dark Chocolate</li>
        <li>Vanilla Ice Cream</li>
        <li>Simple syrup (water, sugar, lemon)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3 style="color: #c0392b; border-bottom: 1px solid #f1e4d1; padding-bottom: 5px;">👩‍🍳 Directions</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Poach the pears in the syrup until tender. Melt the chocolate with a splash of cream to make a smooth sauce. Place a poached pear next to a scoop of vanilla ice cream.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">Pour the warm chocolate sauce over the cold pear and serve immediately.</p>

    <div style="background: rgba(192, 57, 43, 0.1); border-left: 5px solid #c0392b; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px; margin-top: 30px;">
        <h4 style="color: #7d2017; margin-top: 0;">💡 Pro Tips:</h4>
        <p style="font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 0;">Use Bosc or Anjou pears as they hold their shape best during poaching. For an extra touch, add a drop of almond extract to your chocolate sauce.</p>
