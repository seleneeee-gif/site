The best Creme Brulee recipe is the classic French method, using heavy cream, egg yolks, and high-quality vanilla, baked in a bain-marie and caramelized with a torch.

---

### 📋 Ingredients (Serves 4-6)

* **500 ml** Heavy Cream (min. 35% fat)

* **5** Egg Yolks

* **100 g** Granulated Sugar (approx. 1/2 cup)

* **1 Vanilla Bean** (or 1 tsp vanilla extract)

* **For the topping:** Brown sugar or granulated sugar

---

### 👩‍🍳 Directions

1. **Heat:** Pour the cream into a saucepan. Add the vanilla bean (split and scraped). Heat until it almost reaches a boil, then remove from heat and let it steep for 10 mins.

2. **Whisk:** In a bowl, whisk the egg yolks and sugar together until the sugar dissolves (do not over-whisk or make it foamy).

3. **Temper:** Slowly pour the warm cream into the egg mixture while whisking constantly. This prevents the eggs from scrambling.

4. **Bain-Marie:** Strain the mixture and pour into ramekins. Place ramekins in a baking dish and fill the dish with hot water until it reaches halfway up the sides.

5. **Bake:** Bake in a preheated oven at **150-160°C** for 30-40 minutes until the edges are set but the center still wobbles slightly.

6. **Chill:** Let them cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least **2-3 hours** (preferably overnight).

7. **Caramelize:** Just before serving, sprinkle 1 teaspoon of sugar on top. Use a kitchen torch to melt and brown the sugar into a crisp crust.

---

### 💡 Pro Tips

* **Straining:** Passing the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve ensures a perfectly smooth texture.

* **Low & Slow:** The low temperature and water bath are essential to prevent the cream from curdling.

* **The Crust:** A kitchen torch gives the best results.
