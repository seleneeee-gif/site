    <h2 style="color: #f39c12;">🍮 Vanilla Bean Crème Brûlée</h2>

    <h3 style="color: #d35400; border-bottom: 1px solid #f1e4d1; padding-bottom: 5px;">🛒 Ingredients</h3>
    <ul style="margin-bottom: 25px; padding-left: 20px;">
        <li>500ml Heavy Cream</li>
        <li>1 Vanilla Bean (split and seeded)</li>
        <li>100g Caster Sugar</li>
        <li>5 Large Egg Yolks</li>
    </ul>

    <h3 style="color: #d35400; border-bottom: 1px solid #f1e4d1; padding-bottom: 5px;">👩‍🍳 Directions</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Infuse the cream with vanilla on low heat. Whisk yolks and sugar until pale. Slowly pour the warm cream into the yolks, straining the mixture. Bake in a water bath at 150°C for 30-35 minutes.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">Let it chill for 4 hours, then caramelize the sugar on top until dark and crunchy.</p>

    <div style="background: rgba(241, 196, 15, 0.1); border-left: 5px solid #f1c40f; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px; margin-top: 30px;">
        <h4 style="color: #9b870c; margin-top: 0;">💡 Pro Tips:</h4>
        <p style="font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 0;">If you don't have a kitchen torch, you can use the oven's broiler, but watch it every second! The perfect texture should be like cold silk under a hot, glass-like sugar layer.</p>
