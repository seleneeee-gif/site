    <h2 style="color: #d35400;">🍫 Classic Chocolate Soufflé</h2>

    


### 🍫 Ingredients
* **110g** Dark Chocolate (60-70% cocoa)
* **4** Egg Yolks
* **5** Egg Whites
* **55g** Sugar
* **1 tbsp** Flour
* **2 tbsp** Butter (melted)

---

### 👨‍🍳 Instructions
1. **Preheat:** Set your oven to **180-200°C**. Grease the ramekins with butter using **upward strokes**.
2. **Melt:** Melt the chocolate and butter together using a **double boiler**.
3. **Whisk:** Whisk the egg whites with sugar until **stiff peaks** form.
4. **Combine:** Stir the egg yolks and flour into the melted chocolate.
5. **Fold:** Gently fold the whites into the chocolate. *Don't deflate them!*
6. **Bake:** Bake for **8-10 minutes**.

---

### 💡 Tips and Tricks
* **Texture:** 10 minutes is the sweet spot.
* **Rise:** Upward motions help the soufflé "climb".*
