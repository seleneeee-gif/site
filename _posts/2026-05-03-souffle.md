    <h2 style="color: #d35400;">🍫 Classic Chocolate Soufflé</h2>

    <h3 style="color: #e67e22; border-bottom: 1px solid #f1e4d1; padding-bottom: 5px;">🛒 Ingredients</h3>
    <ul style="margin-bottom: 25px; padding-left: 20px;">
        <li>110g High-quality Dark Chocolate</li>
        <li>4 Egg Yolks & 5 Egg Whites</li>
        <li>55g Granulated Sugar</li>
        <li>Butter and sugar for the ramekins</li>
    </ul>

    <h3 style="color: #e67e22; border-bottom: 1px solid #f1e4d1; padding-bottom: 5px;">👩‍🍳 Directions</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Prepare your ramekins by brushing them with butter in upward strokes. Melt the chocolate over a water bath. Whisk the egg whites into stiff peaks while slowly adding sugar. Gently fold the chocolate mixture into the whites without deflating the air.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">Bake at 200°C for about 10-12 minutes until they rise perfectly.</p>

    <div style="background: rgba(230, 126, 34, 0.1); border-left: 5px solid #e67e22; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px; margin-top: 30px;">
        <h4 style="color: #d35400; margin-top: 0;">💡 Pro Tips:</h4>
        <p style="font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 0;">Never open the oven door during baking, or your soufflé will collapse! Also, ensure your egg whites are at room temperature for maximum volume.</p>
